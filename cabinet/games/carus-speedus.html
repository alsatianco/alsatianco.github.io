Carus Speedus

A simple driving game. Drive down the street, avoid other cars, and earn money to buy new ones.

How to play

  • Touch the left side of the screen to turn left, and the right side to turn right.
  • Avoid other cars and the sidewalk.
  • Collect stars to earn more money, and use the money to buy new cars.
