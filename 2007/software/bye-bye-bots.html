Bye Bye Bots

A simple on and off switch for AI in Chrome. Block AI chat websites, and hide AI answer panels in search engines.

What it does

Bye Bye Bots has two protections. You can turn each one on or off.

  • Block AI chat websites. Opening a site like chatgpt.com, claude.ai, gemini.google.com, or perplexity.ai shows a blocked page instead.
  • Hide AI features inside websites. AI overviews, AI panels, and AI chat entry points are hidden in Google Search, Bing, and DuckDuckGo. Hiding AI summaries on YouTube is optional.

You can also add your own blocked sites, keep an allow list, and set an optional PIN, so the settings are harder to change by accident.

Install it

  1. Download the source code and extract the ZIP.
  2. In Chrome, open chrome://extensions.
  3. Turn on Developer mode.
  4. Click Load unpacked, and choose the extracted folder.

Limits

A determined user can turn off or remove extensions, unless the browser profile or the device is managed by a parent or a school. Use Bye Bye Bots together with your device's family controls.

Privacy and permissions

Bye Bye Bots does not sell or share data, has no analytics or advertising trackers, runs no remote code, and does not send your browsing history anywhere. Settings, block lists, and the PIN are stored with chrome.storage.local on your computer.

  • Access to all sites: you can add any website to the block list or the allow list, so the extension must be able to match any site.
  • Declarative Net Request: Chrome's rule-based way to block or redirect a page. It is what makes full-site blocking reliable.
  • Storage: keeps your settings on this computer.
