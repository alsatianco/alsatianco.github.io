How to play

Your village was peaceful, until everyone went crazy. Now rocks fall from the sky, saw blades fly around, and the villagers chase you. How long can you survive?

  • Steer with the joystick. Use your left or your right hand.
  • Avoid rocks, saw blades, and the people chasing you.
  • Collect stars for protection from rocks and people. Stars do not protect you from saw blades or bombs.
  • A bomb explodes soon after you touch it, so use it to trap the people chasing you.
