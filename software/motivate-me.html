Motivate Me! Encourage Me!

A small Android app with hundreds of motivational quotes. A friendly anime character reads them to you out loud, in English.

What it does

Press Motivate Me or Encourage Me, and the character speaks a quote to you, out loud in English, not only as text. The app comes with hundreds of quotes, so you do not need to search for them online.
