Robot Chess Android privacy policy

Alsatian Studio · Updated 24 November 2026

This policy covers the Robot Chess Android app, package com.alsatian.robotchess.

Information stays on your device

The Android app runs entirely on your device. It has no account system, advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs, or network permission. We do not collect or share personal information through the app.

Your settings, saved match, and cached game files are kept in this app’s local storage. You can remove them by clearing the app’s storage in Android settings or uninstalling it. Android cloud backup is disabled.

Children and permissions

The game welcomes children and adults. It does not request names, ages, contact details, location, microphone, camera, or device identifiers.

Outside links and support

Outside links open your browser after an adult check. Those websites have their own privacy policies. Information you voluntarily send by email is used to respond to your request.

Contact: alsatianco@gmail.com.

Google Play and Android may process information under their own policies independently of this app.
