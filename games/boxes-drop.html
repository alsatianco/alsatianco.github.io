Boxes Drop

A pixel-art tower game on a sunny beach. Change the direction of each falling box, and land it on the others.

How to play

Tap the screen to change the direction of the falling box. Land it on the other boxes, not on the ground.

Two versions

Boxes Drop - Tower block has more box skins, 25 badges to earn, experience points, and a beach scene with palm trees, sea, and islands.

Boxus Droppus is the first version. It is free and has no ads.
